# CHORNA VADA — drafts (work in progress)

Andrii's unreleased sketches: no music yet. Unlike `songs.md`, these are NOT final —
editing, rhymes and native-English polishing are welcome **when Andrii asks**.
Also use them as a source of themes and inspiration for new tracks.
Saved exactly as he sent them, including his Russian translations and notes.

Themes here are different from the rest of the catalogue: survival, loss, love, hope —
more personal and less nihilistic. Keep that tone; don't push them toward "Bitter Pill" darkness.
