# Craft reference: rhyme, rhythm, sound

## Rhyme types (from strict to loose)
| Type | Example | Use |
|------|---------|-----|
| Perfect | fire / higher, cage / rage | Choruses, hooks — but overused pairs sound cheap |
| Family (same vowel, related consonants) | cut / stuck / up | Natural, modern, great for screams |
| Assonance (vowel only) | break / pain / saint | The backbone of metalcore lyrics |
| Consonance (consonants only) | blood / bled / bold | Aggressive verses |
| Multisyllabic | dead weight / bed made / hesitate | Makes verses feel crafted |
| Internal | "I *bleed* what I *need*, I *breathe* what you *leave*" | Fast screamed passages |
| Identical / repeat | "Never again / never again" | Breakdowns, gang vocals — deliberate |

Overused pairs to avoid unless twisted: fire/desire, pain/again, heart/apart, soul/control, light/night/fight, die/lie/inside, away/stay, fall/all, sky/fly.

## Rhyme schemes
- AABB — punchy, hardcore.
- ABAB — classic verse.
- XAXA — only lines 2 and 4 rhyme; most natural in choruses.
- Unrhymed screamed verse with a rhymed chorus is completely normal in the genre.

## Syllables and delivery
- Count syllables of the original line and keep replacements within ±1 unless the user says the rhythm is flexible.
- When offering a fix, show syllable count if rhythm is tight: `Burn the bridges you built (6)`.
- Screams: hard consonants (k, t, p, g, d), short vowels, words that can be bitten off.
- Cleans on long notes: open vowels — "away", "alive", "go", "alone", "high", "home". Avoid ending a held note on "-ing", "-ee", "s/z" clusters.
- Breath: no line over ~12 syllables in a fast part without a natural pause.

## Imagery
- Concrete > abstract: not "my pain is endless" but "I still set two plates on the table".
- One controlled metaphor per section; don't mix (fire, then drowning, then a cage, in four lines).
- Callbacks: let the bridge or last chorus twist an earlier line ("I carry you" → "I buried you").
- Titles: short, strong noun phrase or an imperative pulled from the hook.

## Section checklist
- Verse: tells something / advances.
- Pre: tension, fewer words.
- Chorus: one idea, singable, repeatable.
- Bridge: perspective shift.
- Breakdown: 2–8 words, chantable, on the downbeats.
