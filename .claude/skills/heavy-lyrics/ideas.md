## Mute
**Core:** shouting into noise — everyone posts, nobody listens. A scream answered with a like.
Meta angle: the band itself fights the feed for views.
- **Verse 1:** 3 a.m., blue light on the face, a thumb flicking the feed; everyone talks at once.
- **Chorus (hook):** "I scream — you scroll" / "Louder than silence" / "Mute me"
- **Verse 2:** self-irony — I'm part of it too: posting, waiting for numbers.
- **Bridge:** phone off; real silence; my own voice without a screen.
- **Breakdown:** "Scroll! Like! Forget!"
- **Images:** blue light, thumb, swipe, notification, "seen", 15 seconds, echo.
- **Avoid:** "lost in the noise", "the void" — clichés.

## VADA
**Core:** band manifesto. «Вада» = flaw/defect. "Call us broken — that's our name."
Hardcore: "we" instead of "I", slogans, gang shouts.
- **Verse 1:** they called us wrong — too old, too loud, too late, defective.
- **Chorus (hook):** "Born defective — built to last" / "We are the flaw" / "Broken by design"
- **Verse 2:** four names = the acronym (Vasyl, Andrii, Dmytro, Artem); a basement, old gear, Thursdays.
- **Bridge:** one line in Ukrainian — «Чорна вада» — as a signature.
- **Breakdown:** "V! — A! — D! — A!" chanted by the whole band/crowd.
- **Careful:** don't mention why Vasyl and Dmytro left Ukraine unless the band decides to.

## Unsent
**Core:** a message after a fight (wife / friend) — typed and deleted all night.
Contrast with "Father": there it's too late; here there's still time.
- **Verse 1:** night, phone, typing dots, "seen" without a reply; pride vs love.
- **Chorus (hook):** "Typing… deleting…" / "Words I never sent" / "Seen at 3 a.m."
- **Verse 2:** what the fight was really about; both are right, both are wrong.
- **Bridge (spoken):** the text of the unsent message itself — cold, quiet (like the interrogation in Born to Lose).
- **Breakdown:** "Send it! — Delete it!"
- **Ending:** the last line decides — sent or deleted. Leave it open or choose.

## Ghost of Me
**Core:** a conversation with myself at 20 — who I dreamed of becoming vs who I became.
Real story: Andrii started extreme vocals late; the band is the answer to that kid.
- **Verse 1 (the young one):** the dream — stage, mic, screaming, a band.
- **Verse 2 (now):** work, bills, compromises; the dream got quiet.
- **Chorus (hook):** "Ghost of me" / "Would he forgive me?" / "I left him behind"
- **Bridge (dialogue):** young self — whisper/spoken; present self — growl.
- **Twist ending:** "I'm here — late, but here." The song itself keeps the promise.
- **Breakdown:** "Not too late!"
